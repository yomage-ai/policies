Privacy Policy

September 5, 2026

This policy applies to your use of Hamster Keeper. We process personal information under the principles of data minimization, purpose limitation, and security control.

1. Information We Process

Account and login identifiers (Apple ID Token), device and system information, operation logs, order and payment information, and push identifiers such as a JPush alias. Recognition and captured content is used only to provide App features.

2. Purpose of Processing

We process information for authentication, session maintenance, cloud sync, expiry reminders, payment fulfillment, analytics, risk control, audit, and legally required retention.

Drafts primarily remain on this device and do not sync as official items. AI recognition temporarily uploads selected photos and necessary information to object storage and recognition services for processing. Unsaved drafts cannot be recovered by signing in on another phone.

After you enable photo sync and save an official item, its record, original photos, card image, and annotations are stored under your account. Photos use private Cloudflare R2 object storage. Signing out keeps these records and synced photos; signing back in or using another phone restores access. Deleting an item or account schedules associated photos for removal. Photos retained only on an older device are uploaded after you agree; photos already lost before upload cannot be recovered.

3. Sharing and Service Providers

We share information only as necessary with providers such as Cloudflare for private photo storage, AI recognition providers, Apple for payments, and JPush for notifications, subject to data-protection responsibilities. We do not sell personal information.

4. Retention and Location

Personal information is retained only as long as needed for its purpose, or longer where required by law. Account and business records use our backend, and official item photos use private Cloudflare R2 storage. Information may be processed or stored outside your country or region; cross-border processing follows applicable requirements.

5. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, withdrawal of consent, or account closure in the App or by emailing xiaoxiong@yomage.com.

6. Account Deletion and Data Handling

You may delete your account from the Me screen. After verification, we delete eligible personal information and cloud data associated with the account. This is irreversible. Account deletion does not cancel an active subscription.

7. Protection of Minors

Minors should use the App with guardian consent and guidance. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14.

8. Updates to This Policy

We will announce policy updates in the App. Material changes will be presented prominently and consent will be requested where required.

9. Contact and Complaints

For questions about this policy or personal-data protection, email xiaoxiong@yomage.com.
