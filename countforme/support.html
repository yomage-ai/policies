怎样开始训练？

首次打开时选择需要的训练；以后可从“动作”页添加训练，在首页点击“开始”。训练阶段由你按医生或康复治疗师的要求选择，不会按日期自动切换。

免费版与 Pro 有什么区别？

免费版包含全部内置训练，每天可跟练 1 次。Pro 每天不限次数，并提供自定义节奏、自建动作和训练编排、录制口令语音包、Apple Watch 跟练及 PDF 报告。可选永久买断或每 3 个月自动续期订阅；当地价格以购买前商店显示为准。

怎样恢复购买、取消订阅或申请退款？

使用原购买 Apple 账户，在 Pro 购买页点击“恢复购买”。取消订阅请前往 Apple 账户的订阅设置；删除 App 不会取消订阅。订阅除非在当前周期结束前至少 24 小时取消，否则自动续期。退款申请请使用 Apple 官方退款入口，由 Apple 审核处理。

切换 App、锁屏、手表有什么区别？

训练运行时可在后台继续听口令；支持的设备可通过锁屏实时活动与灵动岛查看倒计时。iPhone 后台不支持震动，手腕节奏提醒使用 Apple Watch，手表功能需要 Pro。首次使用请完成配对与权限设置，并在 iPhone 上选好训练；手表也可以开始已收到的训练。首次在手表开始跟练时，请按需允许健康中的“体能训练”权限，支持熄屏后的震动和声音；拒绝授权时退回计时方式，熄屏提醒受限。使用前请先结束其他 App 正在进行的体能训练。App 不读取健康数据，也不向苹果健康保存体能训练记录。

数据保存在哪里？怎样删除？

训练、疼痛自评和自录语音保存在本机，不需要账号。在“设置 → 关于 → 清除所有数据”中清除手机内的记录与自录语音；Apple Watch 上的副本、系统备份以及已导出或分享的文件，需要在相应位置另行删除。

它能替代康复治疗吗？

不能。本 App 是训练计时和计数工具，不提供诊断或治疗建议。请按专业人员要求调整训练；出现明显疼痛或不适时，停止训练并咨询专业人员。

隐私政策 / Privacy policy · Apple 标准使用条款 / Terms of use

Count for me · Help & Support

Contact xiaoxiong@yomage.com with your app version, device model and a description of the issue. You do not need to send medical reports, pain logs or recordings. Hide private information before optionally sending screenshots.

How do I start?

Choose routines during setup or add them from the Exercises tab, then tap Start on Today. Select your stage as advised by your doctor or physical therapist; stages do not change automatically with the date.

Free and Pro

Free includes all built-in routines and one guided session per day. Pro adds unlimited sessions each day, custom rhythms, exercises and routines, personal voice packs, Apple Watch coaching and PDF reports. Choose a lifetime purchase or a 3-month auto-renewable subscription. The store displays local prices before purchase.

Purchases, cancellation and refunds

With the original Apple account, tap Restore Purchases on the Pro purchase screen. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Apple subscription settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Subscriptions renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Request refunds through Apple; Apple reviews requests.

Background, Lock Screen and Watch

Voice cues continue while a session runs in the background. Supported devices can show a countdown on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island. iPhone cannot vibrate in the background; use Apple Watch for wrist cues. Watch features require Pro. Pair the devices, grant the required permissions and choose a routine on iPhone first; a received routine can also be started on the Watch. When first starting on Watch, allow the Health workout permission if you want screen-off wrist and audio cues. Without permission, timing continues with limited screen-off cues. End workouts in other apps before starting. This app does not read health data or save workout records to Apple Health.

Data and deletion

No account is required. Training data, pain self-ratings and personal recordings stay on your devices. Settings → About → Erase all data removes phone records and custom recordings. Remove Watch copies, system backups and exported or shared files separately.

Medical information

The app is an exercise timer and counter, not a diagnosis or treatment service. Follow your clinician's instructions. Stop and seek professional advice if you experience significant pain or discomfort.
