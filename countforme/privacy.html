替我数 隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 11 月 22 日

「替我数」（以下简称「本 App」）是一款康复训练计时与跟练工具。我们非常重视你的隐私，本 App 的设计原则是：不需要账号；训练数据保存在你的设备上，不会自动上传。只有你主动提交意见反馈时，才会发送你写下的内容和你选择附上的图片。

一、我们不收集的信息

本 App 不接入任何广告、统计或第三方追踪服务，不会自动读取你的姓名、手机号、位置或训练记录，也不会读取系统的设备标识；你主动写进反馈或选入附图的信息，按下述反馈规则处理。

二、意见反馈

提交反馈时，我们会接收你填写的内容、选择的图片、联系方式及必要的诊断信息，仅用于处理反馈和排查问题。

三、保存在你设备上的信息

以下信息只保存在你自己的手机（以及你配对的 Apple Watch）上，我们无法访问：

  • 你填写的训练方案信息：首页选择的训练、训练阶段、每天几轮、提醒时间及各训练设置；
  • 训练记录：完成的轮次、时长、你记录的疼痛分数；
  • 你自定义的动作、训练编排和设置；
  • 你录制的口令语音包（录音文件）。

在「设置 → 关于 → 清除所有数据」中可清除手机内的训练数据和自录语音，或通过系统删除 App。已同步至 Apple Watch 的信息、系统备份以及你另存或分享的文件需在相应设备或位置另行删除。你导出的 PDF 报告或语音包文件，只有在你主动通过系统分享发送时才会离开你的设备。

四、权限说明

  • 通知：用于每日训练提醒和冰敷 / 热敷结束提醒，全部是本地通知。
  • 麦克风：仅在你录制自己的口令语音包时使用，录音只保存在本机。
  • Apple Watch 健康权限：为熄屏后的节奏提醒请求写入“体能训练”的权限，运行体能训练会话。App 不申请读取健康数据，不向苹果健康保存体能训练记录，也不向我们上传健康信息。系统可能根据训练会话生成健康指标，具体由 watchOS 管理；拒绝授权仍可计时，但熄屏提醒受限。
  • 后台音频：训练时切换到其他 App 后继续播报口令。
  • 反馈附图：仅通过系统照片选择器访问你选中的图片，不扫描相册，不使用相机。

五、应用内购买

购买 Pro 由 Apple App Store 处理。我们不会获得你的支付信息，只会从商店得知该设备是否拥有 Pro 权益。

六、医疗免责声明

本 App 是训练计时工具，不提供诊断或治疗建议，不能替代医生或康复治疗师。内置方案参考常见的术后居家康复方案，请以你的医生要求为准。

Count for me Privacy Policy

Updated: November 22, 2026

Count for me ("the app") is a rehab exercise timer and coach. It is designed so that no account is needed, and your training data stays on your device and is not uploaded automatically. Information is sent only when you choose to submit feedback.

1. Information we do not collect

The app includes no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking. The app does not automatically read your name, phone number, location or training history, and does not read system device identifiers. Information you choose to include in feedback or attachments is handled as described below.

2. Feedback

When you submit feedback, we receive your message, selected images, contact details and necessary diagnostic information, solely to handle your feedback and troubleshoot problems.

3. Information stored on your device

The following stays on your phone (and your paired Apple Watch). We cannot access it:

  • Plan details you enter: selected home trainings, training stages, rounds per day, reminder times and per-training settings;
  • Training history: completed rounds, duration and the pain scores you record;
  • Your custom exercises, routines and settings;
  • Voice packs you record (audio files).

Use Settings → About → Erase all data to remove training data and custom voice recordings from your phone, or delete the app through the system. Copies synced to Apple Watch, system backups, and files you saved or shared separately must be removed from those locations. PDF reports and voice pack files only leave your device when you share them yourself.

4. Permissions

  • Notifications: daily training reminders and ice/heat timers. All notifications are local.
  • Microphone: only used when you record your own voice pack. Recordings stay on your device.
  • Apple Watch Health permission: requests permission to share workouts so a workout session can support wrist and audio cues while the screen is off. The app does not request access to read health data. It does not save workout records to Apple Health or upload health information to us. watchOS may generate health metrics during a session; the system manages that behavior. Without permission, timing remains available but screen-off cues are limited.
  • Background audio: keeps the voice cues going when you switch to another app during a round.
  • Feedback images: the system photo picker provides access only to the images you choose. The app does not scan your photo library or use the camera.

5. In-app purchases

Pro purchases are processed by the Apple App Store. We never receive your payment details; the app only learns from the store whether your device has Pro.

6. Medical disclaimer

Count for me is a training timer. It does not diagnose or treat any condition and does not replace your doctor or physical therapist. Built-in plans follow common home rehab programs; always follow your own doctor.

Personal information processor: Shanghai Yomage Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd. (Yomage).
