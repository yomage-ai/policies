替我数 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 15 日

「替我数」（以下简称「本 App」）是一款康复训练计时与跟练工具。我们非常重视你的隐私，本 App 的设计原则是：不需要账号，不收集、不上传你的个人信息。

一、我们不收集的信息

本 App 没有服务器，也不接入任何广告、统计或第三方追踪服务。我们不会收集你的姓名、手机号、邮箱、位置、设备标识或任何使用数据。

二、保存在你设备上的信息

以下信息只保存在你自己的手机（以及你配对的 Apple Watch）上，我们无法访问：

  • 你填写的训练方案信息：首页选择的训练、训练阶段、每天几轮、提醒时间及各训练设置；
  • 训练记录：完成的轮次、时长、你记录的疼痛分数；
  • 你自定义的动作、训练编排和设置；
  • 你录制的口令语音包（录音文件）。

在「设置 → 清除所有数据」中可清除手机内的训练数据和自录语音，或通过系统删除 App。已同步至 Apple Watch 的信息、系统备份以及你另存或分享的文件需在相应设备或位置另行删除。你导出的 PDF 报告或语音包文件，只有在你主动通过系统分享发送时才会离开你的设备。

三、权限说明

  • 通知：用于每日训练提醒和冰敷 / 热敷结束提醒，全部是本地通知。
  • 麦克风：仅在你录制自己的口令语音包时使用，录音只保存在本机。
  • 后台音频：训练时切换到其他 App 后继续播报口令。

四、应用内购买

购买 Pro 由 Apple App Store 或 Google Play 处理。我们不会获得你的支付信息，只会从商店得知该设备是否拥有 Pro 权益。

五、儿童隐私

本 App 不面向 16 岁以下儿童，也不会收集任何人的个人信息。

六、医疗免责声明

本 App 是训练计时工具，不提供诊断或治疗建议，不能替代医生或康复治疗师。内置方案参考常见的术后居家康复方案，请以你的医生要求为准。

个人信息处理者：上海有魔法智能科技有限公司（Yomage）。

七、联系我们

如有疑问，请发邮件至 xiaoxiong@yomage.com。本政策如有更新，会在此页面公布。

Count for me Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

Count for me ("the app") is a rehab exercise timer and coach. It is designed so that no account is needed and no personal information is collected or uploaded.

1. Information we do not collect

The app has no server and includes no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking. We do not collect your name, phone number, email, location, device identifiers or usage data.

2. Information stored on your device

The following stays on your phone (and your paired Apple Watch). We cannot access it:

  • Plan details you enter: selected home trainings, training stages, rounds per day, reminder times and per-training settings;
  • Training history: completed rounds, duration and the pain scores you record;
  • Your custom exercises, routines and settings;
  • Voice packs you record (audio files).

Use Settings → Erase all data to remove training data and custom voice recordings from your phone, or delete the app through the system. Copies synced to Apple Watch, system backups, and files you saved or shared separately must be removed from those locations. PDF reports and voice pack files only leave your device when you share them yourself.

3. Permissions

  • Notifications: daily training reminders and ice/heat timers. All notifications are local.
  • Microphone: only used when you record your own voice pack. Recordings stay on your device.
  • Background audio: keeps the voice cues going when you switch to another app during a round.

4. In-app purchases

Pro purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive your payment details; the app only learns from the store whether your device has Pro.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 16 and does not collect personal information from anyone.

6. Medical disclaimer

Count for me is a training timer. It does not diagnose or treat any condition and does not replace your doctor or physical therapist. Built-in plans follow common home rehab programs; always follow your own doctor.

Personal information processor: Shanghai Yomage Intelligent Technology Co., Ltd. (Yomage).

Questions: xiaoxiong@yomage.com. Any updates to this policy will be posted on this page.
